| From | To | Keys | Writer |

### Actions
- `SaveProposal::handle()`: `$data` gains `kind?: ?string` (create only, `Rule::enum(ProposalKind::class)`, default `project`, message `proposals.choose_the_proposal_kind`) and `narrative?: ?string` (payroll, ≥ 50 chars, `proposals.narrative_required`). For payroll, `projectIds`, `beneficiaries`, `startingStage` and `skipReason` are ignored; it creates at the concept note (first note "proposal recorded"), then moves to draft with `proposals.payroll_start_note`. On edit, the stored kind decides; a sent `kind` is ignored.
- `LinkProposalProject`: refuses payroll (`projectIds` → `proposals.payroll_has_no_projects`).
- `TransitionProposal`: passes the kind, adds the `narrative` key, and `hasFinalReport()` skips the milestone for payroll.
- `CorrectProposalStatus`: passes the kind.
- `ApproveProposal`: payroll goes through `approvePayroll()`. It has no linked-project check, and `actual_date_*` default to the planned dates. The evidence rule and decision date are unchanged. It writes `approved` (note + event), then `active` (note `proposals.payroll_active_on_approval`, dated the decision date, + event), and returns an `ApprovalResult` with empty lists (`no_agreement` warning as before). The shared write of `approved` is now `recordApproval()`, used by both paths.
- `CloneProposalForRenewal`: a payroll renewal goes through `clonePayroll()` and lands in `proposal draft`; `existingDraftFor()` looks for a `proposal draft` renewal for payroll (the double-click guard).
- `RecordFinalReportAcceptance`: unchanged (it never needed the milestone).

4. **`targets()` / `correctionTargets()` take the kind**, alongside `requirements()`.
5. **Payroll correction targets:** draft, submitted, active, completed and rejected.

8. **Renewal** stays payroll (narrative, donor, notes, dates; no projects, framework or plan).
9. **W-12 / W-34a exclusions** are deferred to those tasks (neither is on `main`).
10. The prompt's "queries carry `kind = project`" guard is met by **refusals plus fixture counts**: the matrix, targets panel and MealCoverage queries have no proposal in them to filter.
