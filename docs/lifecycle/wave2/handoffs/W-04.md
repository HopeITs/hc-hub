## The nested-modal pattern
`ManagesDonorForm` and `ManagesProposalForm` both declare `$name` and `$notes`, so they cannot share one component. The proposal form therefore hosts a child Livewire component, `<livewire:donors.request-form />` (`components/donors/⚡request-form.blade.php`), included from `pages/proposals/_proposal-form.blade.php` for `donors.create` holders. The "Add donor" button dispatches `open-donor-request`; the child (using `ManagesDonorForm` and `x-donors.form-modal`) saves through `SaveDonor` and dispatches `donor-requested` with `donorId`. `ManagesProposalForm::donorRequested(int)` accepts it only for a donor the user may pick, selects it, and focuses `#proposal-donor`. The pending banner is `[data-donor-pending][role=status]` in `x-proposals.form-modal` (props `donorPending`, `canAddDonor`).
