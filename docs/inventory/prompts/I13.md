# I13 · Disposals and sales with approval

Phase P4 · Model: sonnet · Group G · Needs: I07 · Decisions: D7 + open question "who approves"

## Goal
A device leaves the register only through a request that management approves: scrapped, sold (with price and buyer)
or written off as lost. This replaces the "Extra Laptops" tab and makes every retirement auditable.

## Sources of truth
1. `docs/inventory/contracts.md §2 (disposals), §5, §6 (invariants 3, 6), §7, §8`.
2. An existing request → approve flow in the hub (Hr leave or contract amendment) for the page pattern and notification recipients.
3. `.ai/rules/pages.md`, `ui.md`, `database-migrations.md`, `alerts.md`, `recipients.md`. Handoffs `I06.md`, `I07.md`.

## Start state
- Custody Actions and `ReportAssetCondition` exist.

## Rules
- `RequestDisposal`: asset must be `InStock`, `Broken` or `Missing`, not on an open transfer, with no other
  `Pending` disposal. `Sold` requires price + currency and a buyer (employee or free-text name). `reason` is required.
  The asset status does **not** change on request. Approvers are notified after commit.
- `DecideDisposal`: needs `inventory.approve-disposal`; the requester can never decide their own request (even an admin).
  Approve → disposal `Approved`, asset `Sold` (type Sold) or `Disposed` (types Disposed, Lost); any open custody is
  closed with a system note; all in one transaction with the asset locked. Reject → `Rejected` with a required note; asset untouched.
- A decided disposal is immutable. Price is shown only to `inventory.manage` and approvers.
- Supporting documents (sale receipt, committee minutes) attach as encrypted media on the disposal.
