# I07 · Custody actions and invariants

Phase P2 · Model: opus · Group D · Needs: I04 · Decisions: D7, D8, D9

## Goal
The heart of the module: handing a device to a person or a center, the holder confirming receipt, and taking it back —
as three Actions that keep the register and the history in agreement under concurrency. Plus the buttons on the asset page.

## Start state
- Assets exist with `InStock` default; no `inventory_custodies` table.

## Rules
- `AssignAsset`: asset locked; must be `InStock`, not on an `InTransit` transfer; holder must exist, be active
  (employee not archived) and be in the asset's city unless the actor has `view-all`. Creates the custody
  (`open_marker = 1`), sets asset `status = InUse` + holder columns, notifies the employee after commit.
  `HolderType::Store` is not a valid target (returning is how an asset goes to the store).
- `AcknowledgeCustody`: only the holding employee (employee custody) or a user with `inventory.assign` in the city
  (center/department custody); idempotent (second call is a no-op, not an error).
- `ReturnAsset`: custody locked and open; sets `returned_at, received_by, condition_in`, `open_marker = null`; asset →
  `InStock` + store holder, or `Broken` when `$markBroken`; asset `condition` updated to `condition_in`.
- `ReportAssetCondition`: `Broken ↔ InStock`, `→ Missing`, `Missing → InStock` (found). An `InUse` asset reported
  broken/missing keeps its open custody (the holder is still accountable) — status becomes `Broken`/`Missing` and the
  custody stays open until returned or disposed. Document this in the handoff.
- All four record activity on the asset.
