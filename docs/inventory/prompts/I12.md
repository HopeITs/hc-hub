# I12 · Transfers between cities and centers

Phase P4 · Model: sonnet · Group G · Needs: I07 · Decisions: D4, D7 · Slices: 2

## Goal
Moving devices between cities or centers is a tracked shipment: IT lists what is sent, the destination confirms each
item on arrival, and an asset's location changes only when it is actually received.

1. `docs/inventory/contracts.md §2 (transfers), §5, §6 (invariants 3–5), §7, §8`.

## Start state
- Custody Actions exist; the asset show page has the action-menu slot.

## Rules
- `CreateTransfer`: source = actor's city scope; every asset must be `InStock`, in the source city, and on no open
  transfer; one foreign or invalid id rejects the whole batch. Status `Draft`; reference `TR-YYYY-0001` generated under a lock.
- `SendTransfer`: Draft → InTransit, `sent_by/at`; notifies destination-city users with `inventory.transfer` after commit.
- `ReceiveTransferItem`: actor scoped to the **destination** city; sets `received_at` and moves the asset
  (`city_id`, `center_id`), keeping `InStock`. Idempotent per item. When the last item is received the transfer becomes `Received`.
- `CloseTransfer`: destination closes an InTransit transfer with unreceived items → those assets become `Missing`
  (via `ReportAssetCondition` logic), transfer `Received`. Requires a note.
- `CancelTransfer`: Draft only. An InTransit transfer cannot be cancelled.
- While InTransit, the asset stays in the source city in the register and shows an "In transit to …" badge.

## Stop and escalate

## Do not
